## What went right

The commit-reveal flow was the right call. It kept both the voting and the sealed-bid auction resistant to front-running, and it fit inside a six hour build.

## What I’d redo

I under-tested the edge where the reveal window closes mid-transaction. It never broke in the demo, but it is exactly the kind of boundary that breaks in production.

## Next time

Fewer features, more tests on the time boundaries, and a written list of what the contract is not allowed to do.
